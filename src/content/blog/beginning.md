This isn't my first attempt at having a little corner of the internet. Years ago, there was MSN Spaces. Then came MySpace, and later I tried WordPress. None of them lasted very long. Life got busy, and eventually I stopped writing. For a long time, I still liked the idea of having a space of my own. I just never quite found the right time—or the right reason—to start again.

This time feels a little different. I finally feel like I'm in a good place to create a space where I can write about whatever I'm curious about. Some days, that might be AI. Some days, localization and translation technology. Other days, it might be a hike somewhere in Québec, a place I've visited, something I've learned at work, or simply an idea that I can't stop thinking about.

## Why Astro?

I decided to build this site with Astro. Partly because I wanted to learn something new, but also because I wanted to have some fun building it. I like being able to tweak the layout myself. I can change the typography, adjust the width of an article, move things around, add images, create categories, and change little details whenever I feel like it. It reminds me a little of those old blogging days, when customizing your page was part of the experience. THis time, I get to decide how everything works. There's something satisfying about that: I'm not just writing the posts — I'm also building the little place where they live.

There is a little bit of joy in being both the **writer and the builder**.

## A place to think

I'm not starting this blog because I think I have everything figured out. Quite the opposite, I often understand something better after I've tried to explain it. Writing gives me a reason to slow down, organize my thoughts, and sometimes discover that my original understanding was incomplete or just wrong. That's especially true with AI and technology, where things are changing so quickly that it feels impossible to ever be completely caught up.

So I don't want this to be a blog where I pretend to have all the answers. I'd rather write about what I'm learning, what I'm noticing, and what I'm still trying to understand. Sometimes that might turn into a carefully researched article. Sometimes it might just be a few thoughts about something I found interesting.

Both are fine.

## A little bit of everything

I don't know exactly what this blog will become! Maybe that's what I like about it. It can be a little bit of everything: 

AI and localization. Technology and work. Hiking trails and places around Québec. 🥾 Travel. Things I'm learning. Things I'm building. And probably quite a few things I haven't thought about yet!

The internet is already full of specialized sites.

This one can simply be mine.

## Starting again

Maybe the biggest difference between this blog and the ones I had years ago is that I'm not trying to make it into something bigger than it needs to be. I'm just curious. I want to learn. I want to write things down. And I want to build a little place where those things can live. I don't know if I'll still be writing here regularly a year from now. 

But right now, I'm enjoying the process. And for the first time in a long time, that's reason enough to start.
